    get_snapshot
    Inspect the topology and telemetry.

    Read the signals behind a diagnosis.
    compare_policies

    triage_gpu_health

    Send an action to the gate. Only an operator can approve it.
    get_action_ledger
    Read proposals, outcomes and the audit ledger.
